# 📊 Day 8 – Power BI Visual Insights

This dashboard focuses on transforming KPI metrics into meaningful business insights using visual storytelling techniques.

---

# 📈 Transaction & Loan Insights

## 1️⃣ Loan Default Rate by Loan Status (Donut Chart)

Displays distribution of loans across:
- Active
- Closed
- Defaulted

**Insight:**
- Majority of loans are Active.
- A smaller portion is Defaulted, which helps assess credit risk.
- Closed loans indicate completed lending cycles.

**Business Value:**
Helps financial institutions monitor loan health and identify potential risk exposure.

---

## 2️⃣ Transaction Value by Channel (Bar Chart)

Channels:
- Branch
- UPI
- Online
- ATM

**Insight:**
- Digital channels (UPI & Online) contribute significantly.
- ATM transactions are comparatively lower.
- Branch transactions remain stable.

**Business Value:**
Indicates digital adoption trends and helps optimize channel strategy.

---

## 3️⃣ Transaction Volume Trend (Line Chart)

Shows transaction value trend over time.

**Insight:**
- Initial growth phase followed by gradual decline.
- Seasonal or structural shifts may impact transaction behavior.

**Business Value:**
Useful for forecasting and identifying performance fluctuations over years.

---

# 👥 Customer & Account Insights

## 4️⃣ Average Account Balance Trend Over Time (Line Chart)

Shows how customer balances evolved over time.

**Insight:**
- Steady decline in average account balance.
- Could indicate increased spending or withdrawals.

**Business Value:**
Helps monitor liquidity patterns and customer financial stability.

---

## 5️⃣ Account Status Distribution (Donut Chart)

Categories:
- Active
- Closed
- Dormant

**Insight:**
- Majority accounts are Closed.
- Active accounts represent ongoing engagement.
- Dormant accounts indicate reactivation opportunity.

**Business Value:**
Helps in customer retention and lifecycle analysis.

---

## 6️⃣ Dormant Accounts by Account Type (Bar Chart)

Account Types:
- Current
- Loan
- Savings

**Insight:**
- Current and Loan accounts have equal dormant levels.
- Savings accounts show relatively fewer dormant cases.

**Business Value:**
Helps prioritize re-engagement strategies by account type.

---

# 🎯 Dashboard Design Approach

- Dark theme for professional financial reporting look
- Clear section separation (Transaction vs Customer insights)
- Minimal clutter, focus on insights
- Interactive slicers for dynamic filtering
- Business-first labeling instead of technical naming

---

# 🧠 What I Learned

- Selecting the right chart for the right business question
- Turning raw numbers into visual narratives
- Highlighting insights instead of just displaying charts
- Maintaining visual consistency (colors, layout, alignment)
- Designing dashboards for decision-makers, not just analysts

---

📌 This dashboard moves beyond KPIs and focuses on explaining business performance through visual storytelling.
