# 📊 Day 7 – KPI Dashboard

## 📌 KPIs Explained

### 1️⃣ Total Transaction Value – 14.43M
Represents the total value of all customer transactions within the dataset.

**Business Meaning:**  
Indicates overall business activity and revenue movement. A higher value suggests strong transaction flow.

---

### 2️⃣ Average Transaction Amount – 3.37K
Average value per transaction.

**Logic Used:**  
Total Transaction Value / Number of Transactions

**Business Meaning:**  
Helps understand customer spending behavior per transaction. Useful for identifying purchasing patterns and pricing insights.

---

### 3️⃣ Total Loan Amount – 357.00M
Total amount of loans issued to customers.

**Business Meaning:**  
Shows overall lending exposure. Important for financial institutions to monitor risk, capital allocation, and credit distribution.

---

### 4️⃣ Total Customers – 119
Total unique customers in the dataset.

**Business Meaning:**  
Represents the active customer base. Useful for tracking growth and segmentation strategies.

---

### 5️⃣ Total Accounts – 5K
Total number of accounts held by customers.

**Business Meaning:**  
Indicates product penetration. A higher number of accounts relative to customers suggests strong cross-selling opportunities.

---

### 6️⃣ Average Account Balance – 203.77K
Average balance maintained across all accounts.

**Logic Used:**  
Total Account Balance / Total Accounts

**Business Meaning:**  
Helps assess customer financial strength and liquidity behavior.

---

# 📈 Key Business Observations

- The Total Loan Amount (357M) indicates significant lending operations.
- With 119 customers and 5K accounts, there appears to be multiple accounts per customer.
- The Average Transaction Amount (3.37K) suggests moderate transaction size.
- The Average Account Balance (203.77K) indicates customers maintain relatively healthy balances.
